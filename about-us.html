About Us and Walk In

Welcome to Norma's Arts and Crafts, where creativity and inspiration come to life! We are happy to offer convenient walk-in services for crafters of all ages and skill levels.

Whether you're looking to start a new project, explore your artistic side, or simply enjoy a fun and relaxing activity, our friendly team is here to help.

No appointment is needed—just stop by and let your imagination guide you. We provide a welcoming environment filled with supplies, ideas, and hands-on support. Come visit Norma's Arts and Crafts today and discover the joy of creating something unique!

Norma's Arts and Crafts began in 2013 with a simple goal: to inspire creativity and provide Bay Ridge with a welcoming place for artists, crafters, and families. Originally located on 3rd Avenue, the store quickly became a neighborhood favorite, known for its wide selection of supplies and friendly, personal service.

Over the years, customers of all ages visited Norma's for everything from school projects to creative hobbies, making it a true community gathering place. Although the original location closed its doors in 2022, the passion for art and creativity never faded.

In 2023, Norma opened a new studio space on Colonial Road, creating even more opportunities for classes, workshops, and hands-on artistic experiences. Today, Norma's continues to bring the Bay Ridge community together through imagination, creativity, and a love for making something special.
